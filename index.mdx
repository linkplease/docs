The MetaSmart API lets your CRM, storefront, scheduler, or internal tools send approved WhatsApp templates through the number connected to your MetaSmart workspace.

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  WhatsApp requires an approved template when your business starts a conversation or messages outside the customer-service window.
</Note>

## What you need

- A connected WhatsApp number in MetaSmart
- An approved template in the same workspace
- A MetaSmart API key with the `messages:send` scope
- The recipient's opt-in where required by WhatsApp policy and applicable law
